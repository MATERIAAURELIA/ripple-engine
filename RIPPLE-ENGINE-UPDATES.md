## Vision

The Ripple Engine is an **exploration tool** — not a product to ship, but a practice to live in. The modes are explorations and course foundation. Each mode = a stage of sound development, a concept to explore, and a track/content pipeline for Insight Timer. Working one stage at a time — develop the sound, develop the concept, then move to the next. The instrument builds itself as you explore it.

**Modes → Explorations → Tracks → Course**

### Core Identity
- **Exploration tool** — not a synth you finish. It's a practice.
- **Fibonacci trainer** — training ears and musical intuition to *feel* Fibonacci timing through play, not theory. Internalizing sacred math through practice.
- **Living exploration** — instead of feverishly building every feature, sit with one mode. Play it. Learn what it teaches. Make something from it. The next mode emerges from what you discovered.
- **Mirror for what the body already knows** — Marcus's handpan playing already mimics Fibonacci patterns naturally. The engine makes that visible. Handpan ↔ Ripple Engine feed each other.
- **Stacking functions** — Ripple Engine practice = Fibonacci training = handpan development = IT content = course material. One practice, five outputs.
- **Course foundation** — by the time Marcus teaches this, he won't be explaining concepts. He'll be sharing what he *knows* from the inside.

## Mode 1: Pure Sine

**Concept:** The fundamental. One sine wave, rippling through mathematical decay. No harmonics, no effects — just the pure tone and what emerges from its echoes.

**Controls:** Gap Timing, Echoes, Quantize
**Tuning:** 440Hz / 432Hz / Just Intonation
**Features:** Free mode (polyphonic), velocity sensitivity, MIDI support

**First Track:** "Pure Sines" — uploaded to Insight Timer (Mar 16-17, 2026)
- Marcus's verdict: "it's such a good track" — listening on repeat

## Architecture (as of Mar 17, 2026)

- **Explorer Mode** — instrument identity
- **Simple / Deep-Dive** toggle — accessible by default, power under the hood
- **Player / Gen tabs** — playing vs generative modes
- **Mode dropdown** — each mode unlocks different parameters and sound concepts
- **MIDI Learn** — map any controller
- **Velocity sensitivity** — toggle on/off
- **Quantize + Gap pattern** — fully independent
